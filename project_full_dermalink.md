### 📚 Restored Flagship PM Case Studies & Descriptions
1. **🛵 Swiggy — Workplace Group Ordering**
   - **Problem Statement**: Multiple riders delivering to the same office building cluster; user friction with manual UPI debt recovery among colleagues.
   - **Hypothesis Framework Table**: Payment split conversion (H1), rider wait time reduction (H2), restaurant kitchen batching (H3).
   - **Proposed Solution & UI**: Location-triggered group carts with 6:30 PM cutoff + high-res **Group Order Lobby Mockup** (`/swiggy_screen2.png`).
   - **Metrics & Prioritization Matrix**: Delivery cost reduction per order, AOV lift, group conversion rate.

2. **🟢 Duolingo — New User 7-Day Retention**
   - **Problem Statement**: Diagnosing Day 2–7 habit crystallization window churn (60-75% drop-off).
   - **Business Context**: Retention compounding LTV/CAC dynamics; streaks rewarding existing habits vs building new ones.
   - **Proposed Solution & UI**: 3-screen interactive UI suite:
     - **Screen 1**: Routine Anchor Onboarding (`/duolingo_screen1.png`).
     - **Screen 2**: Micro-Lesson Selector (`/duolingo_screen2.png`).
     - **Screen 3**: Contextual Behavioral Cues (`/duolingo_screen3.png`).

3. **✈️ MakeMyTrip — First-Time Flyer Experience (Tier-2/3 India)**
   - **Opportunity & Market Context**: UDAN regional airport expansion (Bareilly to Bangalore) & jargon anxiety (PNR, layovers, hand baggage).
   - **Proposed Solution**: *"First Flight Mode"* with plain-language fare rules & step-by-step airport walkthrough.
   - **UI Mockup**: High-resolution **Simple View & Fare Rules UI** (`/mmt_screen1.png`).

4. **🌿 Dermalink — Intelligent Multimodal Skincare Auditor**
   - **Executive Summary**: Selfie to 3-zone skincare diagnosis & 3-tier routine generator.
   - **Privacy & Visual Diagnostics**: Client-side eye redaction canvas + **Tri-Zone Facial Moisture Map & Metrics Dashboard** (`/dermalink_zone_map.png` & `/dermalink_metrics.png`).
